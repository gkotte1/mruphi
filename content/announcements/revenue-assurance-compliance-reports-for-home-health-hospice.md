Murphi.ai, an AI-native American healthcare platform, has launched its Revenue Assurance & Compliance Documentation module — a suite of 28 AI-generated compliance reports built exclusively for Home Health and Hospice providers. Covering everything from OASIS accuracy reviews and F2F documentation to CTI validation and the proprietary End-of-Life Risk Band Analyzer (EOLRBA), the platform helps agencies close documentation gaps before they become costly claim denials.

A standout feature is the Evidence Preview Optimizer, which converts Spanish clinical inputs into compliant English documentation in minutes — eliminating translation bottlenecks for agencies serving bilingual patient populations. With AI accuracy reaching up to 98% through a structured human review layer, Murphi.ai reduces compliance effort and cost by half. For Home Health and Hospice organizations navigating CMS audits and revenue leakage, this launch marks a defining step forward in AI-powered post-acute care.

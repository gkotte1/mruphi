Murphi.ai is proud to announce that it has been recognized as a Bronze Stevie® Award Winner at the prestigious 2026 American Business Awards®, one of the most respected business award programs in the United States.

The recognition reflects Murphi.ai’s continued innovation in transforming healthcare workflows through AI-powered automation, intelligent documentation, compliance optimization, and revenue cycle intelligence.

The American Business Awards® honors organizations that demonstrate outstanding performance, innovation, and business excellence across industries. Murphi.ai was recognized for its impact in advancing healthcare AI solutions that help providers improve operational efficiency, reduce administrative burden, and enhance patient care outcomes.

Murphi.ai’s platform supports healthcare organizations across Home Health, Hospice, Behavioral Health, Physician Practices, Specialty Care, and other post-acute and clinical segments with enterprise-grade AI automation solutions.

“This recognition is a reflection of the incredible work our team is doing to bring meaningful and scalable AI innovation to healthcare,” said Guru Tadiparti, Founder & CEO of Murphi.ai. “We remain committed to building practical AI solutions that create measurable value for healthcare organizations.”

The Stevie® Awards organization officially notified Murphi.ai of the award recognition following high average scores received from the judges.

Murphi.ai continues to expand its healthcare AI ecosystem through strategic partnerships, advanced AI workflows, and enterprise-scale automation capabilities designed specifically for the evolving healthcare industry.

Intelligent Documentation Assistance

How Murphi Completes Documentation For You

01 · Before the Visit

Walk in already prepared.

Murphi pulls the referral and existing EHR record ahead of time, pre-filling what's already known — history, diagnoses, prior medications — and gives the clinician a short summary of the packet. No evening spent sifting through discharge paperwork before tomorrow's first visit.

Referral Summary
Discharge packet condensed to one page
Primary diagnosis: CHF, NYHA Class II.
Recent hospitalization: 4 days, discharged stable.
Homebound status: mobility-limited, documented.

Medications Loaded
02 · During the Visit

Hit record. Murphi listens.

Murphi quietly listens to the conversation with the patient, filtering out anything that isn't clinically relevant. If something required gets missed — a wound assessment, a medication reconciliation — Murphi flags it in the moment, while the clinician is still in the room, not after they've left.

Live Encounter
Wound assessment not yet addressedFlagged in real time
03 · After the Visit

Finish it from the car, or the couch.

Anything sensitive or left out can be added afterward with a short dictation, at the clinician's convenience. Murphi combines the visit recording, the dictation and the referral information into the completed OASIS, HOPE or discipline note — ready for review the same day.

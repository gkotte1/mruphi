How Murphi.ai Works

Connect via APIs or Login to Experience AI Automation

Add AI features to your platform via encrypted APIs

Integrate Murphi.ai into your EHR or healthcare platform via encrypted API, SSO, or iFrame. Your customers get AI-powered workflows immediately — under your brand, with zero token cost to you.

  • Encrypted API · SSO · iFrame · FHIR R4 · HL7 v2
  • White-labeled — your brand, your customers
  • Zero token cost — Murphi absorbs AI infrastructure
  • Data and IP remain yours entirely
